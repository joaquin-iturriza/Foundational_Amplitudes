\begin{frame}{Daniel's catalog and what is measured}
  \vfill
  \begin{minipage}{0.97\textwidth}
    \footnotesize
    \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
        \item Daniel's 1491 processes, $2\to2$ and $2\to3$ with proton partons at 13 TeV collider energy, his generator settings, our own events
        \item His split, 1352 training and 139 held out. Interpolation, 96 held-out processes that are a relabel of a training process (swap of massless quarks of equal charge, of $e$ and $\mu$, or charge conjugation). Extrapolation, the 43 with no relabel in training
        \item Every number in this deck is the per-process MSE of $\log|\mathcal M|^2$ (natural log) on 2000 events of that process, with nothing fitted on the held-out process, no rescale and no shift
        \item Level part, the squared mean error, which one number per process would remove. Shape part, the variance of the error. Per process the two add up to the MSE; the plots show the median of each part over processes, and medians do not add up
        \item Full run, $10^5$ events per process and 32k steps. Short run, $10^4$ events per process and about 4k steps, otherwise the same. One seed each
    \end{itemize}
  \end{minipage}
  \vfill
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{What the network is trained to output}
  \vfill
  \begin{minipage}{0.97\textwidth}
    \footnotesize
    \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
        \item \textbf{Per-process standardization}, $\log|\mathcal M|^2$ minus the process's own mean over its own spread. A new process has neither, so both are predicted from the process by a small fit to the training processes
        \item \textbf{One shared standardization}, one mean and one spread for all processes. The network learns each process's level itself
        \item \textbf{$\hat s$ factor, fixed scale}, $|\mathcal M|^2$ times $(\hat s\,\Lambda^{-2})^{N-4}$ with one fixed scale $\Lambda$, its mass dimension for $N$ particles. \textbf{$\hat s$ factor and coupling product}, also divided by the couplings of the leading diagram
        \item \textbf{Dimensionless target}, $|\mathcal M|^2\,\hat s^{\,N-4}$, no units and no fitted scale. \textbf{Pair scale}, the event's pair invariants in place of $\hat s$. \textbf{Dimensionless target and inputs}, also momenta divided by $\sqrt{\hat s}$
        \item \textbf{Coupling orders}, also divided by $(4\pi\alpha_s)^q(4\pi\alpha)^e$ with $q$, $e$ the process's QCD and QED orders
        \item \textbf{Emission scales with coupling orders}, dimensionless target and inputs with coupling orders, where each particle beyond $2\to2$ brings its own soft or collinear scale in place of $\hat s$
        \item \textbf{Context vector}, 16 learned numbers per training process. A new process gets zeros, so zero shot runs without it; it is meant to be fitted on a few labelled events
        \item \textbf{Diagrams}, the Feynman diagram encoder of Daniel's paper. On in every run unless a label says without diagrams
    \end{itemize}
  \end{minipage}
  \vfill
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{The new catalog}
  \vfill
  \begin{minipage}{0.97\textwidth}
    \scriptsize
    \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.15em}
        \item One process per class of processes equal up to relabelling, checked numerically on $|\mathcal M|^2$, so no held-out process is a relabel of a training one
        \item Trees at $2\to2$, $2\to3$, $2\to4$ on every beam ($e^+e^-$, quark pairs, quark and gluon, two gluons), one-loop QCD virtuals, loop-induced processes, two $\alpha_s$ values, a heavy quark with the top's quantum numbers at 50, 300, 600 GeV, a $Z'$ and a two-Higgs-doublet model
        \item Held out, each process with its whole class
        \begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{0.05em}
          \item New combinations, about 5\% of the classes, every particle, multiplicity and order of which is seen in training
          \item Higher multiplicity, the $2\to4$ processes of families whose $2\to4$ is never trained, and $2\to5$ probes
          \item Particle content, every process with a physical top. In a twin run trained without them, every process with a $\tau$ or $\nu_\tau$, and every process with a quartic gauge vertex
          \item One loop, one-loop processes held out while their trees train
          \item $\alpha_s=0.13$, trained at 0.09 and 0.15
          \item Outside the trained kinematics, training processes at $\sqrt{\hat s}$ from 1 to 10 TeV (trained from threshold up to 1 TeV), and at $p_T\geq30$ GeV (trained at 10)
        \end{itemize}
        \item Short runs, 4264 steps over 1397 training pools, one seed each, read on 2000 events per held-out process. The main run is emission scales with coupling orders; the fine tunes start from it
    \end{itemize}
  \end{minipage}
  \vfill
\end{frame}
