- Save through `ps.save(fig, base)`, one file per figure, its panels and legend inside (a spare grid cell:
  `ps.legend_cell`): it writes `.png` and `.pdf` with the same
  basename and `<base>.legend.txt`; never `fig.savefig`. Figures are gitignored (on disk, not committed). Single-format
  exceptions go in `.claude/figure_pair_ignore.txt`.
- Load the figure-style skill before drawing. Binding, whatever a sibling script does:
  - a figure reads without the text: what is compared is named on it in plain words (a legend entry, at most two
    lines of 40 characters), each axis defines its quantity, a scanned parameter is a named colour bar, no internal
    names; paper captions short (figure-style skill), captions sent to Joaquin full (CLAUDE.md);
  - readable but short: one short name per arm everywhere (`from scratch`, `fine-tuned`), $D_{\rm train}$ for the
    training-set size, one short name for a scanned parameter on its colour bar and axis, y label
    `MSE$(\log|\mathcal{M}|^2)$` alone, one-line tick labels;
  - the process label names the process only; strata, cuts, energies and counts go in the caption; no other text in
    a panel;
  - true against predicted distributions are drawn by `ps.true_pred_hist`: the ratio directly under its histogram on
    the same x axis, every event on the frame, the ratio in every bin with true events;
  - data is never clipped, masked or dropped for appearance.
- `ps.save` prints `!!` for each breach it sees; `send_check` refuses a figure with one, one without its legend file
  or drawn by uncommitted code, a panel file or legend strip, and a caption that leaves a symbol entry undefined; a figure of the paper draft goes as made by `tools/draft_figure.py <label>`.
