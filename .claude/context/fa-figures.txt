- Save through `ps.save(fig, base)` (`ps.save_panels`, `ps.legend_strip`): it writes `.png` and `.pdf` with the same
  basename and `<base>.legend.txt`; never `fig.savefig`. Figures are gitignored (on disk, not committed). Single-format
  exceptions go in `.claude/figure_pair_ignore.txt`.
- Load the figure-style skill before drawing. Binding, whatever a sibling script does:
  - a legend entry is a short symbol or formula on one line (at most 30 characters), defined in the caption;
  - the process label names the process only; strata, cuts, energies and counts go in the caption; no other text in
    a panel;
  - true against predicted distributions are drawn by `ps.true_pred_hist`: the ratio directly under its histogram on
    the same x axis, every event on the frame, the ratio in every bin with true events;
  - data is never clipped, masked or dropped for appearance.
- `ps.save` prints `!!` for each breach it sees; `send_check` refuses a figure with one, one without its legend file
  or drawn by uncommitted code, and a caption that leaves a legend entry undefined.
